Our Story

Authentic Gujarati Heritage in Mehsana

Guided by Owner & Chef Mann Prajapati, Maganlal Bhakhri Shak Wala brings authentic Gujarati culinary traditions to Mehsana. Won award of Great gujarati chef, Chef Mann Prajapati crafts homestyle flavors that guests cherish.

Savor the best Indian Thali and spicy Paneer dishes at Maganlal Bhakhri Shak Wala. We bring the heart of Gujarati home cooking to your table — freshly made bhakhri prepared right in front of you, every single day.
